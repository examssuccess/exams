Formula Sheet: Information and Formulae
(1.) The Mathematics of Finance: Simple Interest: $4750 earns $1068.75 simple interest when invested for 4 years 6 months.
Calculate the rate percent per annum.


$ \text{principal, } P = \$4750 \\[3ex] \text{simple interest, } SI = \$1068.75 \\[3ex] \text{time, } t = 4\text{ years } 6\text{ months} \\[3ex] t = 4 + \dfrac{6}{12} \\[5ex] t = 4 + 0.5 \\[3ex] t = 4.5\text{ years} \\[3ex] \text{rate, } r = ? \\[5ex] SI = P \times r \times t \\[3ex] r = \dfrac{SI}{P \times t} \\[5ex] r = \dfrac{1068.75}{4750 \times 4.5} \\[5ex] r = \dfrac{1068.75}{21375} \\[5ex] r = 0.05 \\[3ex] r = 0.05 \times 100\% \\[3ex] r = 5\% $
(2.) Mensuration: The sum of the interior angles of a regular polygon is 2,340°.
Determine the size of an exterior angle.


$ \underline{\text{Regular Polygon}} \\[3ex] \text{side} = n \\[3ex] \text{sum of interior } \angle s = 180(n - 2) \\[3ex] 180(n - 2) = 2340 \quad\text{[Given]} \\[3ex] n - 2 = \dfrac{2340}{180} \\[5ex] n - 2 = 13 \\[3ex] n = 13 + 2 \\[3ex] n = 15 \\[5ex] \text{an exterior } \angle = \dfrac{360}{n} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{360}{15} \\[5ex] = 24^\circ $
(3.) Matrix Algebra: (a.) Find the values of x and y given the operation $ \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ y & -2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}. \\[5ex] $ (b.) If the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & n \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ has a determinant of 10, find the value of n.


$ (a.) \\[3ex] \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ y & -2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \begin{bmatrix} 2(1) + 3(-4) \\ y(1) + -2(-4) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \\[5ex] \begin{bmatrix} 2 - 12 \\ y + 8 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \\[5ex] 2 - 12 = x \\[3ex] x = -10 \\[5ex] y + 8 = 2 \\[3ex] y = 2 - 8 \\[3ex] y = -6 \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \begin{vmatrix} 4 & n \\ -1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 10 \\[5ex] 4(1) - (-1)(n) = 10 \\[3ex] 4 + n = 10 \\[3ex] n = 10 - 4 \\[3ex] n = 6 $
(4.) Exponential Functions: The number of expected views of an influencer's content can be calculated using the formula $v = 1,000 + 3^d$ where v is the number of days the content is published.
(a.) Calculate the expected number of views after 7 days.
(b.) Determine how many days it waill take for the expected number of views to reach 60,049.


$ (a.) \\[3ex] v = 1,000 + 3^d \\[3ex] \text{For } d = 7\text{ days} \\[3ex] v = 1000 + 3^7 \\[3ex] = 1000 + 2187 \\[3ex] = 3187\text{ views} \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] v = 1,000 + 3^d \\[3ex] \text{For } v = 60049\text{ views} \\[3ex] 60049 = 1000 + 3^d \\[3ex] 60049 - 1000 = 3^d \\[3ex] 3^d = 59049 \\[3ex] \log 3^d = \log 59049 \\[3ex] d \log 3 = \log 59049 \quad\text[(Law 5 Exp]} \\[3ex] d = \dfrac{\log 59049}{\log 3} \\[5ex] = 10\text{ days} $
(5.) The Mathematics of Finance: Percent Applications: The sales agents in a store earn $260 per week plus 8% commission on sales over $500.
In a certain week, one agent sells goods valued at $1630.
(a.) Calculate his gross pay for that week.
After a busy week, another agent's gross pay that week was $436.
(b.) Calculate the value of the goods she sold that week.


$ \text{Gross pay for the week} = \text{Basic weekly pay} + \text{Commission} \\[5ex] (a.) \\[3ex] \text{Basic weekly pay} = \$260 \\[3ex] \text{Commission} = 8\% \times (1630 - 500) \quad\text{[8% commission on sales over \$500]} \\[3ex] = \dfrac{8}{100} \times 1130 \\[5ex] = \$90.4 \\[3ex] \text{Gross pay for that week} \\[3ex] = 260 + 90.4 \\[3ex] = \$ 350.40 \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \text{Gross pay for that week} = \$436 \\[3ex] \text{Basic weekly pay} = \$260 \\[3ex] \text{Let the value of the goods sold that week} = p \\[3ex] \text{Commission} = 8\% \times (p - 500) \quad\text{[8% commission on sales over \$500]} \\[3ex] = 0.08(p - 500) \\[3ex] = 0.08p - 40 \\[3ex] \implies \\[3ex] 436 = 260 + (0.08p - 40) \\[3ex] 436 = 260 + 0.08p - 40 \\[3ex] 436 = 0.08p + 220 \\[3ex] 436 - 220 = 0.08p \\[3ex] 0.08p = 216 \\[3ex] p = \dfrac{216}{0.08} \\[5ex] = \$2700 $
(6.) Rational Expressions: Simplify $\dfrac{4}{x - 3} - \dfrac{2}{x + 1}$


$ \dfrac{4}{x - 3} - \dfrac{2}{x + 1} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{4(x + 1) - 2(x - 3)}{(x - 3)(x + 1)} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{4x + 4 - 2x + 6}{(x - 3)(x + 1)} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{2x + 10}{(x - 3)(x + 1)} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{2(x + 5)}{(x - 3)(x + 1)} $
(7.) Factoring: (a.) Factorise completely
(i.) $2ax - bx + 12ay - 6by$
(ii.) $x^2 - 36y^2$

(b.) Hence, simplify $\dfrac{x^2 - 36y^2}{2ax - bx + 12ay - 6by}$


$ (a.) \\[3ex] (i.) \text{ Factoring by Grouping} \\[3ex] 2ax - bx + 12ay - 6by \\[3ex] x(2a - b) + 6y(2a - b) \\[3ex] (2a - b)(x + 6y) \\[5ex] (ii.) \text{ Factoring by the Difference of Two Squares} \\[3ex] x^2 - 36y^2 \\[3ex] x^2 - 6^2y^2 \\[3ex] x^2 - (6y)^2 \\[3ex] (x + 6y)(x - 6y) \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \dfrac{x^2 - 36y^2}{2ax - bx + 12ay - 6by} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{(x + 6y)(x - 6y)}{(2a - b)(x + 6y)} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{x - 6y}{2a - b} $
(8.) Literal Equations: Make m the subject of the formula $b = \dfrac{1 - am^2}{1 + m^2}$


$ b = \dfrac{1 - am^2}{1 + m^2} \\[5ex] \text{Cross Multiply} \\[3ex] b(1 + m^2) = 1 - am^2 \\[3ex] \text{Expand} \\[3ex] b + bm^2 = 1 - am^2 \\[3ex] \text{Collect like terms} \\[3ex] bm^2 + am^2 = 1 - b \\[3ex] \text{Factor by GCF} \\[3ex] m^2(b + a) = 1 - b \\[3ex] \text{Divide both sides by } (b + a) \\[3ex] m^2 = \dfrac{1- b}{b + a} \\[5ex] \text{Take the square root of both sides} \\[3ex] m = \pm\sqrt{\dfrac{1 - b}{b + a}} $
(9.) Applications of Derivatives: Given the curve with equation $y = 3x^2 - 4x + 1$ and the point $P(2, 5)$ on the curve, find
(a.) the gradient of the curve at P,
(b.) the equation of the tangent to the curve at P.


$ (a.) \\[3ex] y = 3x^2 - 4x + 1 \\[3ex] \dfrac{dy}{dx} = 6x - 4 \quad\text{[Power Rule]} \\[5ex] \text{ At Point, } P(2, 5) \implies x = 2,\;\; y = 5 \\[3ex] \left.\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x = 2} = 6(2) - 4 \\[5ex] = 12 - 4 \\[3ex] = 8 \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \text{The equation of the tangent to the curve is a straight line that touches the curve at Point, } P(2, 5) \\[3ex] y = mx + b \quad\text{[Equation of a straight line in Slope – Intercept form]} \\[3ex] x = 2 \\[3ex] y = 5 \\[3ex] m = \dfrac{dy}{dx} = 8 \\[5ex] \implies \\[3ex] 5 = 8(2) + b \\[3ex] 5 = 16 + b \\[3ex] b = 5 - 16 \\[3ex] b = -11 \\[3ex] \implies \\[3ex] y = 8x - 11 $
(10.) Linear Functions: The points $(2, y)$ and $(3, 7)$ are on a straight line.
If the gradient of the line is 4, find the value of y.


$ \text{Point 1: } (2, y) \\[3ex] x_1 = 2 \\[3ex] y_1 = y \\[5ex] \text{Point 2: } (3, 7) \\[3ex] x_2 = 3 \\[3ex] y_2 = 7 \\[5ex] \text{Gradient, } m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \\[5ex] m = 4 \quad\text{[Given]} \\[3ex] \implies \\[3ex] 4 = \dfrac{7 - y}{3 - 2} \\[5ex] 4 = \dfrac{7 - y}{1} \\[5ex] 4 = 7 - y \\[3ex] y = 7 - 4 \\[3ex] y = 3 $
(11.) Geometry:

Two similar cones are shown.
The surface area of the larger cone is 54 cm².
(a.) Find the surface area of the smaller cone.
The volume of the smaller cone is 24 cm³.
(b.) Find the volume of the larger cone.


Scale Factor, Perimeter Ratio, Area Ratio, and Volume Ratio of Similar Figures Theorem
It states that for two similar figures, the ratio of the perimeters is the same as the scale factor; the ratio of the areas is the ratio of the square of the scale factor, and the ratio of the volumes is the ratio of the cubes of the scale factor.

$ \underline{\text{Similar Cones: Left to Right: Smaller Cone A to Larger Cone}} \\[3ex] \text{Height of Smaller Cone} = 6\;cm \\[3ex] \text{Height of Larger Cone} = 9\;cm \\[5ex] \text{Scale Factor} \\[3ex] = \dfrac{\text{Height of Smaller Cone}}{\text{Height of Larger Cone}} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{6}{9} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{2}{3} \\[5ex] (a.) \\[3ex] \text{Ratio of the Surface Areas} \\[3ex] = \dfrac{\text{Surface Area of Smaller Cone}}{\text{Surface Area of Larger Cone}} = \text{Scale Factor}^2 \\[5ex] \implies \\[3ex] \dfrac{\text{Surface Area of the Smaller Cone}}{54} = \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^2 \\[5ex] \text{Surface Area of the Smaller Cone} = 54 \times \dfrac{4}{9} \\[5ex] = 6 \times 4 \\[3ex] = 24\;cm^2 \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \text{Ratio of the Volumes} \\[3ex] = \dfrac{\text{Volume of Smaller Cone}}{\text{Volume of Larger Cone}} = \text{Scale Factor}^3 \\[5ex] \dfrac{24}{\text{Volume of the Larger Cone}} = \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3 \\[5ex] \dfrac{\text{Volume of the Larger Cone}}{24} = \left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^3 \\[5ex] \text{Volume of the Larger Cone} = 24 \times \dfrac{27}{8} \\[5ex] = 3 \times 27 \\[3ex] = 81\;cm^3 $
(12.) Probability: Upon getting home, an eccentric teacher uses a spinner to decide which of four activities to do.
The probability of the spinner landing on each activity is shown below.

Activity Probability
Read a book 0.2
Watch TV 0.1
Grade papers 0.4
Do housework 0.3

(a.) Find the probability that the teacher does not watch TV.
One evening, the teacher decides on two activities using the spinner.
(b.) Find the probability that the teacher
(i.) watches TV then reads a book,
(ii.) grades papers and does housework, in any order.


$ (a.) \\[3ex] P(\text{Watch TV}) + P(\text{Does not watch TV}) = 1 \quad\text{[Complementary Rule]} \\[3ex] 0.1 + P(\text{Does not watch TV}) = 1 \\[3ex] P(\text{Does not watch TV}) = 1 - 0.1 \\[3ex] = 0.9 \\[5ex] (b.) (i.) \\[3ex] P(\text{Watches TV, then reads a book}) \\[3ex] = 0.1 \times 0.2 \quad\text{[Multiplication Rule for Independent Events]} \\[3ex] = 0.02 \\[5ex] (ii.) \\[3ex] P(\text{grades papers and does housework, in any order}) \\[3ex] = P(\text{grades papers, then does housework } OR \text{ does housework, then grades papers}) \quad\text{[Addtion Rule for Mutually Exclusive Events]} \\[3ex] = (0.4 \times 0.3) + (0.3 \times 0.4) \quad\text{[Multiplication Rule for Independent Events]} \\[3ex] = 0.12 + 0.12 \\[3ex] = 0.24 $
(13.) Functions: The function $f(x) = \dfrac{2}{x}$ and the function $g(x) = 2x - 3$.
(a.) Find $gf(4)$
(b.) Find the values of x for which $f(x) = g(x)$


$ f(x) = \dfrac{2}{x} \\[5ex] g(x) = 2x - 3 \\[3ex] (a.) \\[3ex] gf(4) \\[3ex] = g(4) \times f(4) \\[3ex] = [2(4) - 3] \times \dfrac{2}{4} \\[5ex] = 5 \times \dfrac{1}{2} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{5}{2} \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \dfrac{2}{x} = 2x - 3 \\[5ex] x(2x - 3) = 2 \\[3ex] 2x^2 - 3x - 2 = 0 \\[3ex] .......................................... \\[3ex] 2x^2 \times -2 = -4x^2 \\[3ex] \text{Factors are: } x \text{ and } -4x \\[3ex] .......................................... \\[3ex] 2x^2 + x - 4x - 2 = 0 \\[3ex] x(2x + 1) - 2(2x + 1) = 0 \\[3ex] (2x + 1)(x - 2) = 0 \\[3ex] 2x + 1 = 0 \text{ or } x - 2 = 0 \\[3ex] 2x = -1 \text{ or } x = 2 \\[3ex] x = -\dfrac{1}{2} \text{ or } x = 2 $
(14.) Trigonometry:

In the diagram, ABC is a triangle and ADE is a sector.
Find correct to the nearest whole number,
(a.) the area of traingle ABC,
(b.) the area of sector, ADE,
(c.) the area of shaded region BCDE,
(d.) the length BC,
(e.) the size of angle ABC.


$ (a.) \\[3ex] \text{Area of } \triangle ABC \\[3ex] = \dfrac{1}{2} \times |AB| \times |AC| \times \sin\angle BAC \\[5ex] = 0.5 \times 19 \times 17 \times \sin 67^\circ \\[3ex] = 161.5 \times 0.9205048535 \\[3ex] = 148.6615338 \\[3ex] \approx 149\;cm^2 \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \text{Area of sector } ADE \\[3ex] = \dfrac{\text{central angle}}{360} \times \pi \times \text{radius}^2 \\[5ex] = \dfrac{67}{360} \times \pi \times 14^2 \\[5ex] = \dfrac{67 \times \pi \times 196}{360} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{41255.39473}{360} \\[5ex] = 114.5983187 \\[3ex] \approx 115\;cm^2 \\[5ex] (c.) \\[3ex] \text{Area of the shaded region } BCDE \\[3ex] = \text{Area of } \triangle ABC - \text{Area of sector } ADE \\[3ex] = 148.6615338 - 114.5983187 \\[3ex] = 34.06321511 \\[3ex] \approx 34\;cm^2 \\[5ex] (d.) \\[3ex] |BC|^2 = |AB|^2 + |AC|^2 - 2 \times |AB| \times |AC| \times \cos\angle BAC \quad\text{[Cosine Law]} \\[3ex] = 19^2 + 17^2 - (2 \times 19 \times 17 \times \cos 67^\circ) \\[3ex] = 361 + 289 - (646 \times 0.3907311285) \\[3ex] = 650 - 252.412309 \\[3ex] = 397.587691 \\[3ex] |BC| = \sqrt{397.587691} \\[3ex] = 19.93960107 \\[3ex] \approx 20\;cm \\[5ex] (e.) \\[3ex] \dfrac{\sin \angle ABC}{|AC|} = \dfrac{\sin \angle BAC}{|BC|} \quad\text{[Sine Law]} \\[5ex] \dfrac{\sin \angle ABC}{17} = \dfrac{\sin 67^\circ}{19.93960107} \\[5ex] \sin\angle ABC = \dfrac{17 \times \sin 67^\circ}{19.93960107} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{17 \times 0.9205048535}{19.93960107} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{15.64858251}{19.93960107} \\[5ex] = 0.784799177 \\[3ex] \angle ABC = \sin^{-1}(0.784799177) \\[3ex] = 51.7021084 \\[3ex] \approx 52^\circ $
