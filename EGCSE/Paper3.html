Examinations Council of Eswatini
Eswatini General Certificate of Secondary Education
Mathematics
Paper 3 Short-Answer Questions (Extended)

Time: 2 hours 30 minutes
Candidates answer on the Question Paper.
Additional Materials: Scientific calculator, Geometrical instruments

Read These Instructions First
(1.) Write your name, centre number and candidate number in the spaces provided.
(2.) Write in dark blue or black pen.
(3.) You may use a soft pencil for any diagrams, graphs or rough working.
(4.) Do not use staples, paper clips, highlighters, glue or correction fluid.
(5.) Answer all questions.
(6.) If working is needed for any question it must be shown below that question.
(7.) The number of marks is given in brackets [ ] at the end of each question or part question.
(8.) If the degree of accuracy is not specified in the question, and if the answer is not exact, give the answer to three significant figures.
Give answers in degrees to one decimal place.
For π, use either your calculator value or 3.142.
(9.) The total of the marks for this paper is 80.

(1.) Trigonometry: Evaluate $\sin^{-1}(\sqrt[3]{0.9})^2$


$ \sin^{-1}(\sqrt[3]{0.9})^2 = \sin^{-1}(0.9654893846)^2 \\[3ex] = \sin^{-1}(0.9321697518) \\[3ex] = 68.7756058 \\[3ex] \approx 68.8^\circ \quad[\text{to one decimal place}] $
(2.) Fractions, Decimals, and Percents: The ratio of girls to boys in a class is 8 : 7.
Express the number of girls as percentage of the class.


$ \text{girls : boys} = 8 : 7 \\[3ex] \text{sum of ratios} = 8 + 7 = 15 \\[5ex] \text{percentage of the class that are girls} \\[3ex] = \dfrac{\text{ratio of girls}}{\text{sum of ratios}} \times 100\% \\[5ex] = \dfrac{8}{15} \times 100\% \\[5ex] = 53.33333333\% \\[3ex] \approx 53.3\% \quad[\text{to three significant figures}] $
(3.) Vectors: Vectors $\underline{a}$ and $\underline{b}$ are represented on the grid.



(a.) Draw $\overrightarrow{AB}$ on the same grid such that:
$\overrightarrow{AB} = 2\underline{a} + \underline{b}$

(b.) $\overrightarrow{CD} = h\underline{a} + k\underline{b}$
Find the values of h and k.


$ O \text{ is the origin, } (0, 0) \\[3ex] OA \text{ is the position vector of point $A$ relative to the origin} \\[3ex] OB \text{ is the position vector of point $B$ relative to the origin} \\[3ex] OC \text{ is the position vector of point $C$ relative to the origin} \\[3ex] OD \text{ is the position vector of point $D$ relative to the origin} \\[5ex] \underline{\text{Points as Position Column Vectors}} \\[3ex] \underline{\text{1st Line Segment, }} \vec{a} \\[3ex] \text{Initial Point} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \text{Terminal Point} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \vec{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 1 - 2 \\ 4 - 6 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \underline{\text{2nd Line Segment, }} \vec{b} \\[3ex] \text{Initial Point} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \text{Terminal Point} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 5 - 3 \\ 6 - 4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \underline{\text{Scalar Multiplication and Addition}} \\[3ex] 2\vec{a} = 2\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \overrightarrow{AB} = 2\vec{a} + \vec{b} \\[3ex] = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} -2 + 2 \\ -4 + 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \underline{\text{Finding the Terminal Position } B} \\[3ex] \text{Position Vector } \overrightarrow{OA} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{OB} - \overrightarrow{OA} \\[3ex] \overrightarrow{OB} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{OA} \\[3ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 0 + 6 \\ -2 + 4 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] $ (a.) The representation of $\overrightarrow{AB}$ on the same grid is:


$ \underline{\text{Line Segment, }} \overrightarrow{CD} \\[3ex] \overrightarrow{OC} = \text{Initial Point} = \begin{pmatrix} 9 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \overrightarrow{OD} = \text{Terminal Point} = \begin{pmatrix} 12 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \overrightarrow{CD} = \overrightarrow{OD} - \overrightarrow{OC} \\[3ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 12 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 9 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 12 - 9 \\ 5 - 3 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \underline{\text{Scalar Multiplication and Addition}} \\[3ex] h\vec{a} = h\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} -h \\ -2h \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] k\vec{b} = k\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 2k \\ 2k \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] \overrightarrow{CD} = h\vec{a} + k\vec{b} \\[3ex] = \begin{pmatrix} -h \\ -2h \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 2k \\ 2k \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} -h + 2k \\ -2h + 2k \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] \implies \\[3ex] \begin{pmatrix} -h + 2k \\ -2h + 2k \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] -h + 2k = 3 \quad...eqn.(1) \\[3ex] -2h + 2k = 2 \quad...eqn.(2) \\[5ex] \underline{\text{Elimination Method}} \\[3ex] eqn.(1) - eqn.(2) \implies \\[3ex] -h - (-2h) = 3 - 2 \\[3ex] -h + 2h = 1 \\[3ex] h = 1 \\[5ex] 2 \times eqn.(1) \implies \\[3ex] -2h + 4k = 6 \quad...eqn.(3) \\[3ex] eqn.(3) - eqn.(2) \implies \\[3ex] 2k = 4 \\[3ex] k = \dfrac{4}{2} \\[5ex] k = 2 $
(4.) Linear Functions: The equation of a line is $y - mx = 5$
The line passes through (2, 9)
Find the gradient of the line and the y-intercept.


$ y - mx = 5 \\[3ex] y = 5 + mx \\[3ex] y = mx + 5 \quad...eqn.(1) \\[5ex] \text{passes through } (2, 9) \\[3ex] x_1 = 2 \\[3ex] y_1 = 9 \\[3ex] y - y_1 = m(x - x_1) \quad[\text{Point – Slope Form}] \\[3ex] y - 9 = m(x - 2) \\[3ex] y = m(x - 2) + 9 \quad...eqn.(2) \\[3ex] y = y \implies eqn.(1) = eqn.(2) \\[3ex] mx + 5 = m(x - 2) + 9 \\[3ex] mx + 5 = mx - 2m + 9 \\[3ex] 2m = 9 - 5 \\[3ex] m = \dfrac{4}{2} \\[5ex] m = 2 \\[5ex] \text{Substituting the value of $m$ in } eqn.(1) \\[3ex] y = 2x + 5 \quad[\text{Slope – Intercept Form}] \\[3ex] \text{gradient, } m = 2 \\[3ex] y-\text{intercept} = 5 $
(5.) Vectors: $\overrightarrow{OA} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix}, \;\; \overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$

Write $\overrightarrow{OB}$ as a column vector.


$ \overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{OB} - \overrightarrow{OA} \\[3ex] \overrightarrow{OB} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{OA} \\[3ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 5 + 3 \\ 3 + 7 \end{pmatrix} \\[5ex] = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ 10 \end{pmatrix} $
(6.) Inverse of Functions: $h(x) + 14 = x + 36$
Find $h^{-1}(2)$


$ h(x) + 14 = x + 36 \\[3ex] \text{Let } h(x) = y \\[3ex] y + 14 = x + 36 \\[3ex] \text{Interchange $x$ and $y$} \\[3ex] x + 14 = y + 36 \\[3ex] \text{Solve for $y$} \\[3ex] y + 36 = x + 14 \\[3ex] y = x + 14 - 36 \\[3ex] y = x - 22 \\[3ex] \therefore h^{-1}(x) = x - 12 \\[5ex] h^{-1}(2) = 2 - 22 \\[3ex] = -20 $
