Eswatini General Certificate of Secondary Education
Mathematics
Paper 4 Structured Questions (Extended)

Time: 2 hours 30 minutes
Candidates answer on the Question Paper.
Additional Materials: Scientific calculator, Geometrical instruments, Tracing paper (optional)

Read These Instructions First
(1.) Write your name, centre number and candidate number in the spaces provided.
(2.) Write in dark blue or black pen.
(3.) You may use a soft pencil for any diagrams, graphs or rough working.
(4.) Do not use staples, paper clips, highlighters, glue or correction fluid.
(5.) Answer all questions.
(6.) If working is needed for any question it must be shown below that question.
(7.) The number of marks is given in brackets [ ] at the end of each question or part question.
(8.) If the degree of accuracy is not specified in the question, and if the answer is not exact, give the answer to three significant figures.
Give answers in degrees to one decimal place.
For π, use either your calculator value or 3.142.
(9.) The total of the marks for this paper is 120.

(1.) (a.) Statistics: Data Presentation: At a conference, delegates were asked to choose one type of meat for their lunch.
The pie chart represents the number of delegates who chose the different types of meat.



Find the percentage of delegates that did not choose pork.

(b.) Set Theory: A group of 60 people were asked what type of music they liked between Kwaito (K) and Amapiyano (A).
The Venn diagram shows the results.



Find the fraction of people that:
(i.) did not like Kwaito music,
(ii.) liked Amapiyano music.


$ (a.) \\[3ex] 2x^\circ + 3x^\circ + 120^\circ = 360^\circ \quad[\text{sum of angles in a circle}] \\[3ex] 5x = 360 - 120 \\[3ex] x = \dfrac{240}{5} \\[5ex] x = 48^\circ \\[5ex] \text{Sector angle of those who did not choose pork} \\[3ex] = 120 + 3x \\[3ex] = 120 + 3(48) \\[3ex] = 264^\circ \\[5ex] \text{Percentage of those who did not choose pork} \\[3ex] = \dfrac{264^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 100\% \\[5ex] = 73.33333333 \\[3ex] \approx 73.3\% \quad[\text{to three signifcant figures}] \\[3ex] $ (b.) The number of people asked (cardinality of the Universal Set) is 60 people.
Set A (those who like Amapiyano music) is a proper subset of Set K (those who like Kwaito music).
So, everyone that likes Amapiyano music also like Kwaito music.
20 people do not like Kwaito music.
Hence, 60 − 20 = 40 people like Kwaito music.
Of those 40 people, 25 people like Kwaito music but not Amapiyano music
So, 40 − 25 = 15 people like Amapiyano music.

$ (i.) \\[3ex] n(\text{fraction of people that did not like Kwaito music}) \\[3ex] = \dfrac{n(\text{number of people that did not like Kwaito music})}{n(\text{total number of people})} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{20}{60} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{1}{3} \\[5ex] (ii.) \\[3ex] n(\text{fraction of people that liked Amapiyano music}) \\[3ex] = \dfrac{n(\text{number of people that liked Amapiyano music})}{n(\text{total number pf people})} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{15}{60} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{1}{4} $

(4.) Functions: $f(t) = 5t - 19$ and $g(t) = 2t + 3$
(a.) Evaluate $f^{-1}g(3)$

(b.) Find $g\left(\dfrac{1}{2}t - 6\right)$

(c.) Hence or otherwise solve $g\left(\dfrac{1}{2}t - 6\right) = 0$


$ f(t) = 5t - 19 \\[3ex] \text{Let } y = 5t - 19 \\[3ex] \text{Interchange $t$ and $y$} \\[3ex] t = 5y - 19 \\[3ex] \text{Solve for $y$} \\[3ex] t + 19 = 5y \\[3ex] 5y = t + 19 \\[3ex] y = \dfrac{t + 19}{5} \\[5ex] \therefore f^{-1}(t) = \dfrac{t + 19}{5} \\[5ex] g(t) = 2t + 3 \\[3ex] g(3) = 2(3) + 3 \\[3ex] = 6 + 3 \\[3ex] = 9 \\[5ex] (a.) \\[3ex] f^{-1}g(3) \\[3ex] = f^{-1}(9) \\[3ex] = \dfrac{9 + 19}{5} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{28}{5} \\[5ex] (b.) \\[3ex] g\left(\dfrac{1}{2}t - 6\right) \\[5ex] = 2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}t - 6\right) + 3 \\[5ex] = t - 12 + 3 \\[3ex] = t - 9 \\[5ex] (c.) \\[3ex] g\left(\dfrac{1}{2}t^2 - 6\right) \\[5ex] = 2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}t^2 - 6\right) + 3 \\[5ex] = t^2 - 12 + 3 \\[3ex] = t^2 - 9 \\[5ex] g\left(\dfrac{1}{2}t - 6\right) = 0 \\[5ex] t^2 - 9 = 0 \\[3ex] t^2 = 9 \quad[\text{Apply the Square Root Property}] \\[3ex] t = \pm\sqrt{9} \\[3ex] t = \pm 3 $
